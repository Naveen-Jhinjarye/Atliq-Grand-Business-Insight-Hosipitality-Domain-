📊 Project Overview
A data analytics project analyzing revenue and occupancy patterns for AtliQ Grands, a luxury five-star hotel chain operating across India.

## 🎯 SITUATION
The Business Problem:
AtliQ Grands, with 20 years in the hospitality industry, was facing significant challenges:

Losing market share to competitors in the luxury hotel segment
Experiencing continuous revenue decline
Making decisions without data-driven insights
No in-house analytics team to guide strategic decisions

The managing director decided to adopt Business Intelligence to turn the situation around.

## 🎬 TASK
My Role:
Hired as a third-party Data Analyst to:

Analyze historical hotel performance data
Identify key revenue and occupancy trends
Build interactive dashboards for executive decision-making
Provide actionable recommendations for business recovery

## Analysis Scope:

Revenue Analysis - Understanding revenue drivers and optimization opportunities

Occupancy Analysis - Analyzing booking patterns and capacity utilization


⚡ ACTION
What I Did:
1. Data Preparation

Collected and cleaned historical booking data
Created data model using star schema design
Built relationships between fact and dimension tables
Transformed data using Power Query

2. Metric Development

Developed 20+ DAX measures including:

Revenue metrics (Total Revenue, ADR, RevPAR)
Occupancy metrics (Occupancy %, Weekday vs Weekend)
Performance indicators (Realization %, Cancellation Rate)
Week-over-Week trend calculations



3. Dashboard Creation

Designed interactive Power BI dashboards
Added dynamic filters for Property, City, and Date ranges
Created visualizations for:

Revenue trends over time
Occupancy patterns (weekday vs weekend)
Property-wise performance comparison
Booking platform analysis
City-level insights



4. Insight Generation

Identified key business patterns
Highlighted underperforming properties
Discovered pricing optimization opportunities
Analyzed booking platform effectiveness


## ✅ RESULT
Key Insights Delivered:

Revenue Patterns

Identified high and low performing properties
Found optimal pricing opportunities across different room categories
Discovered platform-specific revenue trends


Occupancy Insights

Revealed significant weekday vs weekend occupancy gaps
Highlighted underutilized properties requiring attention
Identified seasonal demand patterns


Strategic Recommendations

Dynamic pricing strategies for revenue optimization
Targeted marketing for low-occupancy periods
Platform-specific booking strategies
Property-level improvement initiatives



Business Impact:

Enabled data-driven decision-making for revenue management team
Provided clear visibility into business performance
Supported strategic planning for market share recovery
Created framework for ongoing performance monitoring
